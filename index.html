      DADLINE

      Dad's Nokia is ringing on the hall table. Caller ID: JOHN MECHANIC. It's about the car! Dad has been waiting for this call all week.

      Dad is somewhere in the house, probably "just checking something". Find him and hand him the phone before John hangs up.

      • ROLL both dice. The Chaos die happens first, then move up to the Move die.
      • Click a glowing tile to walk there. Each turn costs 1 ring.
      • Dark rooms are unsearched. Walk in to look for Dad.
      • Toys block you unless MEGA-BOT, your hero figurine (Chaos 6), saves the day. Two risky shortcuts: the stuck door (walk into it and jiggle the handle) and the laundry chute.

      Space = roll · Enter = stay · M = mute

      MOM NEEDS YOU!

      HOW TO PLAY

      • Each turn: ROLL → Chaos event → click a glowing tile (or STAY). The phone loses 1 ring.
      • Reach Dad's tile to deliver the phone. If the rings hit 0, the call is missed.
      • Walls & furniture block you. Toys block you unless MEGA-BOT, your hero figurine, clears the way.
      • Every game is a new house. Rooms connect to each other in different ways each time, so look for loops!
      • Stuck door (the door with the brass keyhole): a big shortcut. Walk into it (orange tile) to jiggle the handle: 4–6 it opens for good, 1–3 it's jammed (−1 ring) and you can try again later. MEGA-BOT opens it for sure.
      • Laundry chute (the two hatches with a down arrow, in rooms far apart): stop on one end to slide across. It's risky: there's a 1 in 3 chance you get stuck (−1 ring).
      • Yellow tiles mean MEGA-BOT has to help you get there (that uses up his help).
